## Problem 1 (True or False)

For each of the following, write **TRUE** if the statement is true and **FALSE** if the statement is false. No explanation is needed.

a) If

$$
f:[0,1]\to(0,2]
$$

is surjective, then $$f$$ cannot be continuous.

b) If

$$
f:\mathbb{R}\to(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)
$$

is surjective, then $$f$$ cannot be continuous.

c) Every continuous function

$$
f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}
$$

has an absolute maximum.

d) If $$f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$$ is continuous, then the image

$$
f((0,1))=\{f(t):0< t<1 \}
$$

must be open in $$\mathbb{R}$$.

e) If a function is differentiable at $$c$$, then it is continuous at $$c$$.

f) If $$a_n\to0$$, then the series

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n
$$

converges.

## Problem 3

c) Prove that $$f: [0,\infty)\rightarrow [0,\infty)$$ defined by $$f(x) = \sqrt{x}$$ is uniformly continuous on $$[0,\infty)$$.



## Problem 4

a) Carefully state Rolle's Theorem.

b) Prove Rolle's Theorem. You may use the Extreme Value Theorem and the theorem that an interior local extremum is a critical point.

c) Carefully state the Mean Value Theorem.

d) Prove the Mean Value Theorem using Rolle's Theorem.

## Problem 5

a) Write down the definition of convergence of the infinite series

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n
$$

in terms of its sequence of partial sums.

b) Prove that if

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n
$$

converges, then $$a_n\to0$$.

c) Give an example showing that the converse of part b is false. Carefully explain why your example works.

d) For $$N\geq1$$, let

$$
s_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac1{n(n+1)}.
$$

Prove directly that

$$
s_N=1-\frac1{N+1},
$$

and use the definition from part a to find

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{n(n+1)}.
$$

## Problem 6

a) Let $$f$$ be a real-valued function on an open interval $$(a,b)$$. Write down what it means for $$f$$ to be differentiable at $$c\in(a,b)$$.

b) State the Product Rule for derivatives. Be sure to include all required assumptions.

c) Suppose that $$f$$ and $$g$$ are differentiable at $$c$$. Prove the Product Rule directly from the definition. You may use the Helper Theorem.

d) State the Quotient Rule, including its required assumptions. You do not need to prove it.

## Problem 8

a)

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n}{n+1}
$$

b)

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{n^2+\sin^2 n}
$$

c)

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n}{n^2+1}
$$

d)

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{n^2}{3^n}
$$

e)

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
\left(\frac{2+(-1)^n}{4}\right)^n
$$

f)

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{\sqrt n}
$$
is continuous if and only if it is constant.
